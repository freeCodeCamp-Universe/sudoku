# --instructions--

You have reached the end of the Getting Started module.

## What to do next

1. **Replace the placeholder panel** — Follow the instructions in `architecture.md`
   to build your interactive component.

2. **Add a real engine** — Implement `LessonEngine<S>` in
   `src/curriculum/lesson-engine.ts`. The `StubEngine` marks everything as
   completed immediately; a real engine grades based on learner input.

3. **Write your lessons** — Add lesson files to `src/curriculum/lessons/` and
   register them in `src/curriculum/ordering.ts`. Run `pnpm add-module` and
   `pnpm add-lesson` to generate stubs.

4. **Set your donation ID** — Replace `"TODO"` in `donation-config.json` with
   your project's donation ID. The `HeaderControls` test will fail until you do.

Good luck!
