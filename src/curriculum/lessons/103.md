# --instructions--

Every sudoku rule is about three kinds of group. Each group is called a **house**, and each
house holds the digits 1 to 9 exactly once.

<img src="/images/learn/houses.svg" alt="Three 9 by 9 grids side by side. The first shades the fifth row, the second shades the fifth column, and the third shades the center 3 by 3 box." width="404" height="168">

## Seeing a cell's houses

Every cell belongs to one row, one column, and one box at the same time. The cells that share
any of those houses are its **peers**, and a digit placed in the cell rules that digit out for
all of them.
